About & Methodology

How data is organized, how to cite sources, and how we keep the tone respectful.

Purpose

Awaaz is an educational project that organizes women’s issues by province and city, and connects each issue to relevant government initiatives and official resources.

Data rules

  • Do not invent statistics. If a number is unknown, mark it as “Add verified statistic”.
  • Every statistic must include a source name and date (report/year) in the “source” field.
  • Keep language factual and non-graphic. Summarize at a policy/statistical level.
  • Ensure each issue links to a real initiative where possible (law, program, helpline, commission, authority).

Suggested sources

Examples of acceptable sources include Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS), UNICEF Pakistan, HRCP reports, ministry pages, provincial commission sites, and reputable news reporting with dates.

Disclaimer

This site is for educational purposes only. Content is compiled from public sources as of a stated date and may be incomplete. Users should verify information with official references.
